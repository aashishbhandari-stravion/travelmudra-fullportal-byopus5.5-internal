# WP-09 Assistance and report a problem

Package record. Owner: the lane architect. Status: designed; architect's decisions taken; independent design review
pending.

## 1. Scope

TA-07p Report a problem (new task page on the existing POST path), the TA-07 help and problem-report panels, OP-02a
Approved assistance (entry, the operator's assistance upload, the ending "Return for TA review" through WP-07's OP-03r),
OP-02r Resolve a hold, SA-09 kinds `assistance_approve`, `assistance_decline` and `flag_response`, the SA-01 and SA-02
lists that link to them, and the old SuperAdmin case page (forms leave it). Five commands: `core.decide_assistance`,
`core.record_assistance_entry`, `core.report_problem`, `core.resolve_hold`, `core.respond_flag`. Proofs V07 (full), BU-21
and BU-22 to their boundary.
