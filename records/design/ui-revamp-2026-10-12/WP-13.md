### 4.1 Independent design review (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, read-only, 13 October 2026)

Full text: lane `research/reviews/WP-13-design-review.md`. 13 findings: 12 P1, 1 P2, no P0. One review, no follow-up (LD-13).

structural update needs no second launch approval; suspension "continue" lets drafts submit; a missing treatment holds
controlled work; withdrawal refuses draft submission; narrowing cannot widen; lift restores the recorded audience; the host

criterion exception, -02 and -06 are projections, -03 to -05 are defects. Amendments bind the implementation and govern
where they differ from `WP-13-design.md`.
