# WP-17 Group end, withdrawal, deferral, timing

Package record. Owner: the lane architect. Status: designed; architect's decisions taken; independent design review
pending.

## 1. Scope

TA-05 group end (TA-05c close the group), TA-07 task pages (TA-07d price decision, TA-07w withdraw, TA-07c held
correction), OP-02 task pages (OP-02f forecast, OP-02d propose a deferral, OP-02e end it, OP-02w applicant withdrawal),
SA-09 kinds (`deferral_approve`, `deferral_decline`, `deferral_extend`, the price increase, the operational withdrawal),
SA-04g group end view, the in-place travel-date recheck; amendments to WP-06, WP-07, WP-08, WP-09 and WP-11. Thirteen
commands. Proof V19 (group-end part; WP-16 owns the financial follow-up part).

values, the TA review date).
