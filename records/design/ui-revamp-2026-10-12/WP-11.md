(LD-13). Reviewed against product f78e558. Confirmed sound: the unresolved command records the outcome, notice and future
owner without completing the case; concurrent decisions are revision-protected; non-concluding attempts move no revision
(F-10); current assigned operators reconcile after rule changes; the posted-value identity mapping; correction stages

criterion); F-22 to F-27, E-16, E-17, D-14, D-15, H-15. Amendments bind the implementation and govern where they differ
from `WP-11-design.md`.
