## 4. Reviews

### 4.1 Independent design review (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, read-only, 12 October 2026)

Full text: lane `research/reviews/WP-08-design-review.md`. 12 findings, all P1, no P0. One review, no follow-up (LD-13).
Amendments bind the implementation and govern where they differ from `WP-08-design.md`.

| Finding | Disposition (amendment) |
|---|---|
| R-01 blind confirmation by a reviewer who cannot see a candidate's identity | **E-01.** A candidate whose identity facts are hidden from the reader shows "Evidence unavailable to you" and is not selectable for confirmation; the reviewer may reject or ask the case's operator. Open until P-13 item 2 is served. |
| R-02 V13 needs a false surname attestation | **E-02.** The confirm proof uses a synthetic result document with visible identity text that automatic extraction cannot establish; the nameless document is kept for the reject and unresolved-review paths. |
| R-03 correction path after completion removed | **E-03.** An incident-scoped replacement form (existing `incidentId` input) stays reachable from the incident for its owner or reviewer after the case completes. |
| R-04 manual-email selections truncated | **E-04.** The handler reads every submitted selection and refuses more than Core's limit with the values kept; never truncates. Test with eleven selections. |
| R-05 completion wording invents evidence | **E-05.** Neutral prerequisite labels and Core's returned flags and codes only; no claim that a notice was posted or an artifact exists unless Core says so; no "Not applicable yet" before an outcome where Core reports a blocker. |
| R-06 owner table assigns duties wrongly | **E-06.** General recording roles are separated from the actual owner; "you" only when the reader is the owner Core names; delivery wording by channel (no "through Send result" for packet or direct external delivery); missing owners and duties qualified ("may be incomplete"). |
| R-07 send-preview eligibility uses the reader's check | **E-07.** The interim label is "Available for your download" with the channel named; TA email eligibility only from Core's evaluation (P-13 item 3, P2). |
| R-08 P-13 overstates the Kind 1 criterion | **E-08.** P-13 amended: item 2 becomes a bounded exception request; items 3–7 stay P2; items 8 and 9 added. |
| R-09 selection results lack version identity | **E-09.** Item numbers only, with the file-identity limit stated; P-13 item 8. |
| R-10 capped lists presented as complete | **E-10.** Actual limits disclosed (notices 200; email attempts first 100); counts qualified; no "none", "latest" or "all" beyond the returned slice; P-13 item 9. |
| R-11 safety-check item contradicts asynchronous processing | **E-11.** Synchronous format and size refusals are separated from an asynchronous safety failure; the latter asserts a preserved outcome, withheld bytes and a truthful processing state. |
| R-12 checklist not reconciled with the design | **E-12.** The Product Manager aligns the named rows to the decisions and the design, adds a corrections table and one ordered recipe; wording bans are scoped by fact and channel ("Delivered" allowed for a truthful direct external delivery); "product owner" is the approved owner wording. |
