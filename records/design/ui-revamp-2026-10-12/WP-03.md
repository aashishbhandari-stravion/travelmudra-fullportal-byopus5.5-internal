## 4. Reviews

### 4.1 Independent design review (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, read-only, 12 October 2026)

Full text: lane `research/reviews/WP-03-design-review.md`. 14 findings: 2 P0, 12 P1. One review, no follow-up (LD-13).
Each disposition is an amendment that binds the implementation and governs where it differs from `WP-03-design.md`.
Seal route verdict (orchestrator's condition): the reviewer found the authenticated encryption, session and target
binding and expiry reasonable and raised no P0 against the seal; R-06 (P1) asks for a closed, fully specified variant.
The seal is adopted with amendment C-06.

| Finding | Rank | Disposition (amendment) | Owner |
