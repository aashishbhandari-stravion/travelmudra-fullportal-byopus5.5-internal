## 4. Reviews

### 4.1 Independent design review (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, read-only, 13 October 2026)

Full text: lane `research/reviews/WP-10-design-review.md`. 14 findings: 1 P0, 12 P1, 1 P2. One review, no follow-up
(LD-13). Reviewed against product e9e96e3. Confirmed sound: the rebuild with `supersedePacketId`; competing rebuilds;
exact-constituent download checks without substitution; packet and physical handover effects; direct-external counting
by the declared delivery kind; receipt discrimination; F-16 to F-21 and E-13 to E-15 keep the intent of the earlier
amendments. Amendments bind the implementation and govern where they differ from `WP-10-design.md`.

| Finding | Rank | Disposition (amendment) |
|---|---|---|
| R-01 dependency enforcement remains bypassable | **P0** | **H-01.** Worked now by escalation: P-15 item 1 to Max through the orchestrator at once (recommendation: the Kind 2 blocker). It is existing product behaviour; a risk for release, not for UAT (synthetic data only). Acceptance separates UI acceptance from enforcement: the V12 dependency clause reads "Not met: waits for Max's decision on P-15 item 1". The UI interim (travel agent "Not linked" on TA-07 and in the TA-08 review) stays; it does not claim enforcement. |
| R-02 link and unlink guard parity impossible from the facts | P1 | **H-02.** No exact-parity claim. The design lists each interim discrepancy (a fact the page cannot see, such as a possible filing on a returned case, shows the form and Core refuses with its reason re-rendered; cycle, preparation-only and relation-specific guards; unlink has no lifecycle guard in the command, so the page adds none). The parity test asserts that the page never hides a served action where the facts allow it and lists the known discrepancies. Core-owned offers stay STOP-W10-05 (P2). |
