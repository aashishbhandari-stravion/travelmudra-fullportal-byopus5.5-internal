# WP-10 Service shapes and dependency

Package record. Owner: the lane architect. Status: designed; architect's decisions taken; independent design review
pending.

## 1. Scope

The service shapes as the operator and the travel agent read them (TravelMudra files; preparation only; provider
handoff; direct external), the packet pages OP-04p (build and rebuild) and OP-04r (release review), the delivery record
pages OP-06h (handover) and OP-06e (direct external delivery), TA-07l Related applications (link and unlink), and
amendments to the WP-03, WP-07 and WP-08 designs. Six commands: `ops.request_packet_build`, `core.release_packet`,
`dlv.record_handover`, `dlv.record_direct_external`, `core.link_dependency`, `core.unlink_dependency`. Proof V12.
