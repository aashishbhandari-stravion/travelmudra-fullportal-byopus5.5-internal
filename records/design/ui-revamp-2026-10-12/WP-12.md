## 1. Scope

original received (`ops.record_original_receipt`), OP-09m record a custody change (`ops.record_original_movement`),

custody block of the SA-03c reassignment preview (`core.reassign` stays WP-06's); the travel agent's "Original
documents" section; amendments to the WP-06, WP-07 and WP-08 designs. Proof V18 to its boundary.
