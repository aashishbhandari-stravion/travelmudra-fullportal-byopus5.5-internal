### 4.1 Independent design review (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, high effort, read-only, 12 October 2026)

Full text: lane `research/reviews/WP-07-design-review.md`. 12 findings: 1 P0, 11 P1. One review, no follow-up (LD-13).
Amendments bind the implementation and govern where they differ from `WP-07-design.md`.

| Finding | Rank | Disposition (amendment) |
|---|---|---|
| R-01 an authorization receipt can invite a repeated act | P0 | **F-01 (class fix).** No act-related text tells the operator to perform an act from a historical receipt or a status read. The authorized state reads "Authorized; execution not yet evidenced." The manual-step instruction reads "Perform the act only if it has not been attempted. If you attempted it, record the evidence now, or mark the execution uncertain." Receipts stay factual (what was recorded). Tests: replay of the authorization receipt after evidence was recorded, after execution was marked uncertain, and after reassignment — none shows an instruction to act. |
| R-02 the fee-control decision lacks a decisive test | P1 | **F-02.** Max's decision (hide the separate fee act while the submission's filing is unknown) applies at every operator authorization entry point, including a direct GET of the authorization review. Test: an unknown non-fee submission, then a separate fee act — no form, the reason shown. Core unchanged. |
| R-03 unresolved authorization protected at one address only | P1 | **F-03.** The BFF keeps the unresolved operation references of the session's authorize requests in bounded session memory (per session, a small cap, expiry) and consults `core.operation` at every authorization entry point; while the original is in progress or unresolved, no authorization form is shown — the page says the earlier request is being checked and links to its status. Test: navigate away while the original is in progress. |
| R-04 an evidence outage leaves the review usable | P1 | **F-04.** When the evidence the authorization review needs cannot be read (availability failure), the case facts stay and the authorization form is removed with a notice (as WP-03 C-05). Test distinct from an access refusal. |
| R-05 the gate basis cannot support payment labels | P1 | **F-05.** The UI never maps a passed gate to a money fact; "No blocker now" plus the exception records. P-14 item 1 amended to ask for the recorded basis per gate and act. |
| R-06 provider handoff labelled as filing | P1 | **F-06.** Evidence labels, banners and receipts are specific to the act kind; a provider handoff establishes handoff only and names the separate provider-filing evidence path; test that a handoff never claims government filing. |
| R-07 future deferrals in "Action now" | P1 | **F-07.** An active deferral sits in "Waiting" until its recorded review is due; the same mapping drives the page and its navigation count. |
| R-08 declining a stale increase states the wrong ceiling | P1 | **F-08.** The decline effect reads the current ceiling from `pay.ta_finance`; the request's old amount is labelled as the amount when requested. Tests: decline and withdrawal after a lowering. |
| R-09, R-10, R-11, R-12 checklist and proof expectations | P1 | **F-09 to F-12.** The Product Manager aligns: the applicant withdrawal request is accepted and recorded with a refused assessment (not a command refusal); reconciliation has no stale-state guard (live state, current authority) — no expectation of an older-state rejection and no new guard proposed; after uncertain execution and reassignment the readiness reads Core's actual state (`stale_basis`), with the original recorder asserted separately and a separate unchanged-basis fixture for readiness-exception eligibility; rows A48, A61, A64, A79, §0.4, §5 aligned to the design and decisions; one ordered recipe with a fresh travel agent per run; tests for all four readiness kinds (scope, eligibility, expiry and revocation, no clearing of other blockers). |

The Product Manager's alignment (F-09 to F-12, plus F-01 to F-08 coverage) is in `WP-07-acceptance.md` (172 items;
corrections table at its top). Two changes beyond the named rows are accepted: A30 runs as a test only (no proof
application keeps an unchanged readiness basis through a reassignment), and the unpaid breach at the default ₹0 and the
breach after a lowering run as tests rather than live checks.
