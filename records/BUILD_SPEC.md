# TravelMudra build specification

Status: Approved implementation specification for the initial new-case B2B product. This document states the implementation authority. TA means TravelAgent; SuperAdmin means TMSuperAdmin; Core means the logical Application Core. A rule applies only within its stated service mode and approved offering. `EXCLUDED`, `PARKED`, `KNOWN_LIMITATION` and `OWNER_DECISION` are deliberate boundaries; leave them visible and continue the supported build. An unresolved route is unavailable for use until its required policy and evidence exist. Do not infer a default to make it launchable.

## Product and admission

**B01 — Product surfaces.** Provide a public, unauthenticated page at `b2b.travelmudra.co` with static TravelMudra details and supported countries; public search covers only that static content. Provide authenticated TA and operator/SuperAdmin surfaces with a common home, country/service discovery, application list, progress, notices, account/payment history and assigned-work/administration views. The applicant has no account or portal. Each visible offering states its cost, processing estimate, required documents and process overview. The public pages need crawlable rendered HTML, unique titles/descriptions, clean canonical HTTPS URLs, sitemap, robots rules and structured data using verified business locality. Keep private case and live catalogue data out of public content. Verify crawling and search readiness before public release.

**B02 — Population and rollout.** Serve TA-managed Indian ordinary-passport holders resident in India for tourism/business visa purposes. Student, diplomatic and work visas are outside the initial scope. Candidate countries are planning inputs, not approved live routes. The first rollout accepts only new TravelMudra cases; historical/in-flight imports and integration with other applications, including Tally, are EXCLUDED. A country/service can be offered only after its current route, eligibility, authority, pricing, evidence, operating readiness and launch requirements are met.

**B03 — Independent work unit.** A group belongs to one TA and one country. An application is one applicant, one selected service and one group, with its own snapshot, authority, price, assigned operator, state, outcome and history. A row may create several independent applications. Duplicate scope is applicant + group + service, without a fourth route key or reusable applicant master. Links and common context do not propagate corrections/outcomes or permit cross-group document reuse. Service changes require fresh application/intake; country changes require a new group. A later same-service attempt in an open group requires completed prior work, explicit fresh intake and authority, current rules and eligibility; completion alone never authorizes repeat external filing.

**B04 — Supported service shapes.** Support declarations for TravelMudra-filed, preparation-only and third-party-filed services. Only the assigned operator files manually for TravelMudra-filed work. Preparation-only ends with a validated packet handed through the TA, holds no originals and claims no later government result. A declared provider, not TravelMudra, files in third-party mode; handoff is not filing. Electronic and physical evidence/custody paths both shape the system. The TravelMudra operator retains declared follow-up, delivery and closure duties after provider handoff. A structural shape does not approve any particular route, provider, government authority or launch.

**B05 — Offering identity and authority.** Maintain separate versioned structural declaration, effective business baseline and compatible released-behavior binding for each TA-selectable offering. Declare promise, eligibility, actors/actions, authority, controls, documents, dependencies, cut-offs, local work/progress mapping, financial conditions, originals, outcome/delivery/completion evidence and operating readiness. An unsupported, incomplete or unresolved required declaration or missing/incompatible binding blocks dependent use. An engineering release with attributed commit approves the structure/behavior it delivers and supplies its binding; an unreleased commit does not. Structure without a supporting release needs the product owner's outside-product approval, recorded with evidence by a SuperAdmin for the exact version/scope. A change to the shared business contract or a new actor/authority shape needs the product owner's separate decision. SuperAdmins may maintain supported effective business values without routine code deployment or a second catalogue approver. They cannot alter shared controls, security, retries or executable behavior through business values.

**B06 — Publication and existing cases.** Keep exact governing identities and meanings for each accepted attempt/event; no universal latest lookup or group freeze. Catalogue changes retain actor, before/after, reason, recorded/publication time, effective condition, affected scope and accountable review. Preview price/impact before publication. Recheck affected unfiled work; unknown applicability prevents filing readiness. Unaffected work continues. A group travel-date edit rechecks affected unfiled applications for validity, readiness and communicated timing; filed facts remain historical. Filed/protected facts remain historical. Existing work receives explicit supported continuation, SuperAdmin-approved per-application structural transition with engineering compatibility evidence and renewed TA authority where required, or an owned hold. Engineering under the product owner supplies each shared-capability release’s affected offering/application scope and business impact through Catalogue to the accountable SuperAdmin for affected-work review. Unresolved impact prevents affected filing readiness. A release does not silently migrate a case. A service/country identity switch still uses fresh intake. A structural change to an already launched, unsuspended offering takes effect for new cases at deployment without a second version-specific launch approval.

**B07 — Launch control.** One individually attributed SuperAdmin role approves and records offering launch/scope expansion, suspension and lifting with actor, reason, evidence and scoped readiness. the product owner's account has the same role permissions. Lifting restores only previously approved scope. An effective withdrawal stops new selection, intake and submission. Already-submitted unfiled work continues only if feasible and the withdrawal expressly permits it; otherwise hold it. Payment cannot force a withdrawn service. Catalogue availability and launch/suspension are separate; a published entry, release or completed survey is not launch approval. Without current scoped approval, supported binding and absence of suspension, refuse TA selection and all new intake, including drafts; truthful information display may continue. Existing commitments, attempts, payment facts and duties survive suspension. If existing-work treatment is missing, pause affected unfiled preparation, packet release and filing until a SuperAdmin records compatible treatment. Where withdrawal and suspension conflict, enforce the more restrictive applicable treatment per action. Reconciliation, original return, incidents and valid delivery continue under their normal checks.

## Identity, access and controlled content

**B08 — Individual identity.** Every account represents one person; no shared credentials or impersonation. Operators and SuperAdmins use MFA. TA sign-in uses email plus short-lived, single-use OTP bound to account/contact and attempt, with protected verifier, bounded attempts and revocable session. Do not disclose account existence or OTPs in errors/logs. Enforce server-side CAPTCHA before TA OTP issuance, bot detection/rate limits at issuance and verification, and bot/request controls before expensive authenticated intake. A passed bot check or verified contact grants no unapproved role, scope or business permission. Protect state-changing requests against CSRF and invalid request origins; expose no generic table-mutation route. Account eligibility, fraud and first-admin/loss-of-all-admin policy remain unselected.

**B09 — TA email change.** Only the signed-in TA can initiate a routine change of that account's email. Require fresh OTP at the current address and OTP at the proposed address bound to the same change. Keep current sign-in and document recipient unchanged until both proofs succeed; then switch both together, record the event, revoke existing sessions and require new-address sign-in. SuperAdmin cannot overwrite the address. Lost-old-mailbox recovery is unavailable until The product owner approves a separate rule.

**B10 — Current object access.** Authorize every metadata read, search, source, direct link, support view, background result, command and byte release against current account state, owning TA, assignment and any exact scoped read grant. TA sees only its work. Assigned operator acts only on assigned applications. A rare named group read grant is purpose/duration limited, revocable and read-only; it confers no case action or cross-TA access. SuperAdmin exact-result support view uses the selected user's read scope without impersonation or target actions. Full audit history is SuperAdmin-only. Revocation, suspension and reassignment take effect on old links, jobs and operation-result lookups. Secondary copies, diagnostics, caches, indexes and backups preserve isolation and block status.

**B11 — Privileged authority.** SuperAdmin manages individual accounts with reason and records approval, activation, suspension, restoration, assignment, catalogue values, manual payment entries, allowed action-gate exceptions and operational queues. Creating/elevating a SuperAdmin normally requires a second distinct existing SuperAdmin; emergency suspension may be unilateral. Credential recovery resets rather than administrator viewing/choosing credentials. Suspending an operator revokes access and transfers duties only through audited reassignment; suspending a TA preserves applications. No initial-admin or total-admin-loss bypass is inferred.

**B12 — Private bytes and release.** Keep documents as immutable, scoped, private versions with integrity evidence separate from association. A content hash never grants membership, verification or access in another scope. An accepted availability claim requires durable, integrity-checked bytes. Missing/unreadable bytes remain unavailable with an owner. Storage and links must not become independent bearer access. Before each file send, including every bulk selection and document email, check current recipient authorization, exact version, positive application/type association, current eligibility and incident block. A block/revocation/replacement effective at that check denies bytes. Record actual retrieval/recipient/version and per-selection included/unavailable/failed results; do not substitute another artifact or alter outcomes. OWNER_DECISION: a change after the check through that file's send is an accepted interval; no send lock, streaming recheck or recall promise is required.

**B13 — Artifact association and disclosure.** Official result and artifact are different facts. Automatic association needs extracted matching name and passport number, correct service/type, no contradictory strong identifier, one uniquely eligible application and stored evidence for that exact version. Missing/conflicting/multiple candidates need a scoped secondary review and owned association incident. The assigned operator owns that incident by default; if none is assigned, a SuperAdmin assigns a reviewer without filing authority. Final delivery uses stored verified association metadata, not a new OCR or LLM guess. Replacement invalidates earlier eligibility. `issued` with missing/wrong artifact retains outcome but withholds delivery and keeps a next action. On confirmed wrong-recipient disclosure, block/noncurrent the version, preserve known retrieval/recipients, assign correction and truthful notices to affected TAs; request deletion without claiming recall. Incident closure needs actual correction/notice evidence even after service completion. Applicant, regulator and legal notice requirements need separately authorized compliance work; do not invent or send them.

## Intake, application work and controls

**B14 — Safe individual and bulk intake.** Individual forms and bulk proposals pass the same Document Processing and Core acceptance. Bulk sources are a public Google Sheet with public links, an uploaded Excel file with public links, or a public folder with/without consolidated Sheet/Excel, supplied for an existing one-country group. Public accessibility is neither consent nor association. Preflight unsafe/unreadable batch prerequisites before application mutation. Map irregular inputs, ask only consequential ambiguity, check files before extraction, and preserve accepted independent rows when another fails; a systematic mapping fault pauses all affected work. One applicant row listing services is preferred; multiple rows per applicant are accepted only when used consistently across a batch, and mixed patterns are not guessed through. A row may yield several drafts with missing requirements if applicant/group/service are unambiguous. Bulk intake never submits, assigns or files.

**B15 — Document evidence.** Applicant documents are initially PDF/JPEG/PNG and at most 5 MB each; this is not the Excel or aggregate-source limit. An offering may narrow formats. Verify format, size and malware before reading content. At processing time, a relevant document must pass the offering’s declared validity checks. Mandatory types require accessible, sufficiently readable, correctly classified and positively associated evidence; optional evidence cannot substitute for a different missing type and its conflicts remain visible. A document about another person may be supporting evidence only where the offering requires it and never becomes the applicant identity document. Passport-based identity uses passport as primary reference; strong-identifier conflict and same-name ambiguity remain unresolved, never name-only/probabilistic merge. Local processing/manual resolution is the starting position. Third-party vendors must not receive customer data; hosted OCR/LLM sharing is EXCLUDED. Unrelated files are excluded with minimal reason and safe temporary disposal.

**B16 — Attempts, corrections and incidents.** Each intake attempt has one applicable baseline and historical row/file results. Fully processed means checks ran, not correctness, submission or filing readiness. Correction uploads link to the original attempt, touch only changed/failed explicitly matched scope, leave omitted rows untouched and route new applicants to separate intake. Nth identical retry resolves accepted effects and eligible failures without duplicate applications or stale overwrite. A correction is all-or-nothing with current revision, evidence, before/after and actual actor. Ambiguous applicant, document, group, service or duplicate stays an owned incident; TA source correction/abandonment or reasoned SuperAdmin dismissal does not cancel an existing application. Batch failure cannot erase accepted rows.

**B17 — Submission and price.** The TA explicitly submits each application after its own review and actual authority/consent confirmation, or separately requests help. Record confirmation presence or absence truthfully. Bulk completion and assistance do not fabricate submission. Formal TA submission fixes that application's one-time quoted price and starts its original estimated window, independently of payment; show later forecast separately. Unpaid price reduction applies automatically; increase requires renewed TA authority, and a declined mistaken-price increase honors the communicated price. Paid amounts and adjustment/refund decisions stay separate. Changed service/material scope needs fresh intake or renewed authority as applicable. Notify the TA when timing threatens travel or materially changes expectations; require acknowledgement only when the TA must decide. An estimate is guidance, not an SLA without a later decision by the product owner.

**B18 — Assigned editor and assistance.** A submitted unassigned case waits in the SuperAdmin assignment queue. SuperAdmin may approve/decline help, then manually assign one accountable operator per application; an existing assistance assignment survives later submission. Payment Gate 1 must pass or be excepted before any operator work, including help. Only the assigned operator contacts the applicant. Ordinary operator action does not change application information or documents: return the case to the TA for correction or flag it to SuperAdmin. Approved assistance is the sole operator information-entry exception, followed by formal return for TA review/submission. On an assigned case that has not been formally returned, TA may read and report a problem only. The product report changes no application data; separate withdrawal, submission, material, response or price-authority actions wait for formal return. A reported, returned-to-TA or flagged-to-SuperAdmin application cannot receive filing authorization or packet release until the assigned operator deals with the issue and reconfirms readiness. Return retains the same assigned operator; that operator makes the ordinary final readiness judgment before filing or packet release and reconfirms it after correction. A SuperAdmin cannot make that judgment or file. SuperAdmin may exceptionally correct from evidence with identity/reason, never invent facts or bypass validations. No extra pre-filing lock is required.

**B19 — Assignment and correction protection.** SuperAdmin may assign singly, by selection or SuperAdmin-initiated round robin; no autonomous or group assignment. Reassignment at any assigned stage records old/new actor, acting SuperAdmin, time and reason, transfers all unresolved action duties, revokes old action authority and does not move originals. New operator reconfirms readiness before another filing unless the readiness gate is separately excepted. Draft TA correction of legal name, date of birth, nationality, passport number or issuing country needs clear same-person evidence. Submitted unassigned correction invalidates affected readiness. Assigned unfiled correction pauses filing/packet release until accepted evidence and renewed readiness. Filed, protected, released or completed facts remain historical; externally permitted remedy or owned incident is separate. No latest-write-wins.

**B20 — Control kinds.** Shared tenets cannot be overridden; mandatory safety/identity/artifact/custody validations require corrected evidence; a fixed evidenced cut-off is not a gate. A SuperAdmin may make only an allowed, reasoned, audited exception for one application, named gate and action. It authorizes that action without asserting payment, TA confirmation, prerequisite, readiness or external fact, without forcing execution, and without clearing validation or extending to a later gate. Unavailable required evidence cannot pass. Check current actor, scope, assignment, applicable rules, revisions, evidence and control state when accepting consequential work.

**B21 — Outcomes and work ownership.** Keep lifecycle, outcome, common progress, external/payment/custody/delivery facts and next owner/action separate. Common TA progress meanings are draft, TA action needed, with TravelMudra, external wait, delivery/closure pending and completed; local steps may loop/overlap. `pending_on_action` names owner/reason/next action. Exceptional `deferred` needs operator justification, SuperAdmin approval, TA notice, review point and monitoring. Only Core records lifecycle/outcome/completion. The allowed outcomes are `issued`, `refused`, `withdrawn`, `cancelled`, `dependency_failed`, `prepared_and_handed_over` and evidenced `external_outcome_unresolved` where applicable. `completed` means normal processing ended, never success by itself. Do not invent cancellation eligibility.

**B22 — Dependencies.** Only declared service relationships condition another application's action/outcome; require the declared upstream outcome and exact verified artifact where specified. Missing evidence is an unmet prerequisite with owner and next action, not terminal failure. An initially unassigned wait belongs to SuperAdmin; later assigned wait retains its operator. `dependency_failed` follows an incompatible final upstream outcome only for a confirmed-unfiled dependent. Filed/filing-unknown dependents keep protection and reconciliation. Preparation-only packet handover does not prove government-result prerequisite. Shared capacity/original constraint does not propagate a sibling outcome.

**B23 — External action and uncertainty.** The assigned actor manually performs the declared external act. Before an irreversible act, durably accept actor, scope, governing evidence, action authorization, protection and reconciliation owner; authorization is permission, not execution evidence. A failed commit means no authorized act. From a possible authority-submitting action, protect attempted facts as if filed until evidenced reconciliation; timeout, payment, handoff, courier receipt, reassignment and elapsed time prove neither filing nor non-filing. Hold proposed corrections and catalogue impacts separately while filing is unknown. No blind repeat payment/filing or ordinary withdrawal while filing may have occurred. Positive non-execution evidence returns through current rules/readiness before a fresh authorization. Known receipt is filing even if progress stalls. If reasonable reconciliation is exhausted, SuperAdmin may authorize truthful administrative `external_outcome_unresolved` completion with attempts, reason, TA notice and future owner; it never grants safe refiling; applicable completion prerequisites under B24 still apply. A later official fact appends owned follow-up.

**B24 — Withdrawal and closure.** Existing electronic TravelMudra filing permits ordinary withdrawal only before evidenced government-portal submission; each other offering must declare its own evidenced cut-off. Eligible TA, evidenced applicant-through-operator or evidenced SuperAdmin operational request is assessed under assignment/editor and uncertainty rules. Accepted withdrawal stops new filing, preserves history, originals, money and remaining duties; it cannot be silently reversed. Closure uses applicable positive outcome, delivery and physical-duty evidence. An open operational incident alone does not hold completion, but an artifact block/failed validation still applies. A physical original holds completion until operator/SuperAdmin records owner receipt. For a TravelMudra-held electronic result, document-carrying email to the owning TA plus web notification evidence hold completion; a TA download does not. A manual operator email requires attributed record and is not claimed system-verified. Direct external delivery records known provenance/recipient/version and truthful absence of a TravelMudra-held result. Delivery/send and completion invocation details under B46 remain unselected.

**B25 — Group end and continuing duties.** Ordinary group closure requires every application completed and every intake incident disposed. An unpaid application with no operator work ever authorized stays open, even after an unworked withdrawal/cancellation/dependency failure, until its group's travel return date passes. Use a declared, tested clock/date basis and timer source for this date-triggered closure and periodic temporary cleanup. Then close only eligible open applications as ended with the group, retaining history; this is not deletion or success. Never close one with unknown filing, held original, pending delivery or outstanding money through this rule. A completed application or closed group may retain linked operational/disclosure incidents and financial follow-up without restarting government processing. Same-service later attempt remains barred while prior work is open.

## Money, custody, delivery and operations

**B26 — Payment Gate 1.** SuperAdmin records manual per-application service payment status and additive corrections with actor/time/evidence. Before assignment/operator work, including approved assistance, require current `payment_received` or a fresh application-specific Gate 1 exception. Assignment is not payment. A correction preserves original gate/action/external history. If work proceeds unpaid under an exception, or a payment entry is later corrected after operator work, retain an owned financial exception and follow-up. No gateway, TA-wide credit or refund portal is approved.

**B27 — Government fees and Gate 2.** For TravelMudra-operator-filed government work, TravelMudra pays every government fee from its account and recovers the exact government amount from the TA despite payment delay or outcome. Track fee, application, TA liability, TM-paid amount, TA receipt/allocation and outstanding recovery separately from service charge and fixed submission price. Before TM pays a government fee or operator submits to government, whichever first, recheck that application's current payment. If unpaid, require a fresh reasoned audited per-application SuperAdmin Gate 2 decision. Gate 1 never satisfies Gate 2. Other service modes gain no inferred Gate 2, TM fee funding or payer rule.

**B28 — TA-wide exposure.** Before a TM-funded government-fee commitment, atomically compare the proposed fee plus that TA's counted reserved and TM-paid-unrecovered fees across every group/application with the TA's rupee ceiling; reserve before commitment, once per logical effect. Core action authorization and that reservation MUST commit together or not at all. Default ceiling is ₹0. A breach is a non-overridable stop, including after Gate 2 exception; zero does not itself bar Gate 1 preparation. One SuperAdmin may lower a ceiling; an increase needs a second distinct existing SuperAdmin, with identities, old/new values, reason and effective time. Lowering below incurred exposure stops new spend without erasing it. Exposure decreases only on Accounts-verified allocated bank receipt; correction/reversal restores it and its owner. Other receivables stay separate. PARKED: which payment record excludes a fee from counted exposure, the ordinary paid case at the default ₹0 ceiling, and the unused-reservation release/authority remain unavailable. Do not infer a count/release rule from payment status, timeout, write-off or completion.

**B29 — Accounts boundary and financial follow-up.** Accounts has individual operator accounts with an additional accounting grant and owns bank-receipt verification/allocation, recovery and manual adjustment duties. Exact dashboard data/actions and Payment Records mutation path are PARKED; do not grant case access or payment mutation by implication. An open financial exception remains listable across applications as `Payment_Missing_Override`, including corrected-payment-after-attempt, work under an exception with no receipt, and worked-before-attempt then corrected-to-unpaid cases. Accounts’ reconciliation responsibility nudges the accountable SuperAdmin while that follow-up remains open. It survives service completion until collection or authorized write-off, without moving the completion event or fabricating payment/override history. Write-off authority/evidence, refunds, assistance charging, price-versus-government-fee formula and other-mode fee policies remain unselected.

**B30 — Physical originals.** For a declared physical route, record each original once with positive association, holder/location or explicit uncertainty, item-level receipt, partial movements, linked applications, return/recipient evidence and surviving duties. Assignment, scans, parcel labels and courier receipt do not prove possession, return or government acceptance. One application's withdrawal/closure cannot erase another duty. Preparation-only holds no originals. Same-group item linkage grants no other operator's application view; cross-TA/group reuse/access remains forbidden. PARKED route policy includes shared-item return priority, assignment suitability, recipient evidence, loss/damage and cross-party visibility; affected steps remain unavailable until bounded/settled.

**B31 — Notification and delivery truth.** TA Delivery records exact application, recipient, version, channel action and actual result for document email, web notice, retrieval and packet/physical handover. Use the owning TA's current account email, never group contact/applicant/arbitrary request address. Sending, notification, retrieval and TA acknowledgement are different facts. A failed/uncertain channel result is not success. No automatic resend from an uncertain email result; a deliberate manual resend is newly attributed and rechecks release eligibility. An external authority's direct delivery may leave no TravelMudra-held download. No transport/provider selection or vendor-data exception is implied.

**B32 — Operational facts and measures.** Record first-live-case facts by offering/service-mode application cohort with sample sizes: stage events and calendar/TM-controlled waits by reason/owner; post-submission correction, repeat external act and wrong-association quality; operator/TA and observable partner effort with sampling/missing shares; row-level intake yield; and per-TA reserved/paid-unrecovered fee count/age/value. Preparation-only uses packet handover and no invented government result. Unresolved outcome, missing effort and degraded reporting are neither refusal nor zero. Deduplicate source effects. Monitor unpaid overrides, unowned waits, travel-date risk, external uncertainty, custody/artifact incidents and reporting gaps. Before each offering launch, set each measure's applicability, owner, numerator/denominator, event boundaries, timezone, waits/pauses, dimensions/exclusions/missing treatment and provisional numeric target, plus support hours, escalation, staffing, peak capacity, availability/response/recovery targets and fallback. Review first live baseline after 30 days with sample-size/missing-data limits. The former 5× ambition is not a product target.

**B33 — Audit and truthful degradation.** Commit mandatory actor, source/evidence, reason, subject, change and relevant event/recorded time with each consequential fact. Preserve original entries and before/after facts; do not infer causality solely from clock order. Required evidence unavailable means no claimed accepted action. Mark retrospective reconstruction and leave absent facts absent. Derived views/search/audit projections may lag or be rebuilt from business truth; they never grant permission or invent values. Continue case processing through projection outage only if all required owner evidence/current controls still work. Non-emergency privileged elevation waits for audit recovery; emergency containment remains available. Technical diagnostics use allowlisted identifiers and error categories; diagnostics, health and administration output exclude source URLs, document content, credentials and extracted personal data. Preserve scoped access.

## System correctness, local proof and deliberate limits

**B34 — Sole writers and seams.** Identity & Access owns accounts/grants; Catalogue owns definitions/bindings/impact; Core owns group/application facts, control acceptance, assignment, action authorization, lifecycle/outcome/completion; Intake owns attempts/rows/incidents; Document Processing owns safety/extraction verdicts; Operations owns local case work, launch, incidents, physical custody and artifact eligibility; Payment Records owns money/exposure/follow-up; TA Delivery owns actual delivery/notice evidence. Operations owns local/shared/unresolved failure classification and its evidence; unknown or shared hosting does not establish a shared-failure exemption. Derived Views owns only projections. Country behavior and workers return scoped proposals/evidence through the writer; they never acquire a second writer or direct cross-owner mutation. Shared passport, upload, authentication, payment check and configuration capabilities have one implementation across offerings.

**B35 — Atomicity and retry.** Consequential acceptance uses current actor, scope, applicable version/rules, evidence/revisions and operation identity in one invariant-preserving commit with required history and durable internal continuation. Guard all competing mutators, including empty duplicate scope, offering admission/impact, assignment, dependency/artifact, completion and TA-wide exposure. Same key/content resolves the accepted result under current read access; same key/different content conflicts. Lost commit acknowledgement is unresolved until the writer reconciles the original operation. A new key cannot evade business duplicate checks. Internal tasks may run at least once but must preserve stable owner effect, bounded retry, failure visibility and original identity; they cannot replay government action/payment. Stale or revoked work cannot inherit old permission. Projections are not guard authority.

**B36 — Content and country failure.** Bound TA-supplied public retrieval by destination/redirect and actual connection; reject local/private/metadata addresses and credential forwarding. Quarantine files; use separate restricted workers for fetch and offline parsing/OCR with scoped temporary files, no direct business write or parser outbound network. Apply time, size, memory and temporary-space limits; terminate timed-out child work, retain scoped failure, and stop affected temporary admission before storage exhaustion. Cleanup must not remove referenced canonical/staged content or a staged file whose owner reference is not yet committed. Country-specific behavior uses scoped inputs, versioned declarations and proposal-only outputs; an ordinary country-local error or mapping failure cannot interrupt or corrupt unrelated countries or erase their common list/queue facts. Review adapter code for memory leaks, runaway work and improper dependencies; retain bounded inputs/results and request time limits. Attribute a country-origin defect to that country even if its resource failure interrupts the shared process. OWNER_DECISION: in-process runaway adapter work may interrupt the shared process; rollback, triage and fix are the response. Do not build a sandbox, broker or per-country scheduler for this accepted risk; no proof of uninterrupted country B during such exhaustion is required. Unauthorized data/write access remains a defect.

**B37 — Local test installation.** Build for the product owner's local server with one active release and authoritative transactional structured state, private scoped bytes and separate restricted file workers. Inventory listeners/resources before assigning private sockets or IP/ports; keep data, secrets and runtime identities separate from other applications. Keep secrets out of source, build output, logs and ordinary backup metadata. Use only the product owner’s authorized local access path; do not open a public tunnel. Remote testers use HTTPS and secure revocable sessions. A synthetic identity fixture seed is permitted only for local testing; it grants no product first-admin or all-admin-loss exception. Use clearly labelled synthetic external/notification outcomes for testing; they do not prove real delivery, CAPTCHA, payment or government action. No production deployment, DNS activation, provider, topology, paid resource or numeric production availability/RPO/RTO commitment is selected. Production resources/configuration follow the product owner's full testing and acceptance.

**B38 — Bounded capacity.** Before local test admission, record finite limits and observed capacity for requests/connections/locks, source/aggregate intake, workers, queues, temporary storage, streams and diagnostics. Begin file proof with one active fetch and one parser; raise concurrency only after measured safety/responsiveness. Reserve host/database administration headroom. At a limit, return truthful unavailable/retryable status, retain accepted work and owner, and protect required business evidence. Separate entry, command, database, byte, worker, cleanup, backup and projection health. Country count is not a measured capacity target.

**B39 — Release and rollback.** A release identifies exact source, dependencies, artifact digest, nonsecret configuration, schema, supported declarations/bindings and worker contract. Local cutover closes admission and byte/work claims, drains old writers, checkpoints consistent data, migrates with distinct authority, verifies actual artifact/schema/binding/worker compatibility while closed, then deliberately opens. Maintenance survives restart; old writers cannot reconnect. Preserve original job/case identities and business approval/suspension state. Code rollback keeps current data and requires compatibility; an old snapshot is allowed only for a proven still-closed cutover with no accepted work since the checkpoint. Otherwise use the restore boundary. An incompatible old binary stays unavailable pending compatible repair. No automatic external action or delivery replay follows cutover.

**B40 — Backup and restore.** A local recovery set closes writers and captures a consistent authoritative database export plus every referenced canonical and still-referenced quarantine blob byte, inventory/digests, schema/binding/config/key prerequisites and explicit gaps. Incomplete capture cannot replace last good set. Encrypt and restrict backup/key custody; same-host copies prove local restore practice only. Restore into isolated namespace, closed ingress/byte/outbound/internal work, new secrets and unusable old sessions/OTP/job credentials. Preserve damaged state and missing facts. An interval-loss restore ends closed; there is no reconciliation-only state, staged reopening or scoped technical hold. Quarantined bytes restore as quarantined with the same access restrictions; backup inclusion grants neither clearance nor publication. Under the product owner's narrow authority, engineering may record only an attributed pre-reopening protection mark through the existing logical owner for applications possibly filed/paid/released during the lost interval; no fact fabrication, exposure release, generic business edit, automatic resend or blind repeat. Unknown affected identity/scope keeps the whole installation closed. The mark does not repair lost contacts, grants, revocations or artifact blocks. The product owner decides any reopening only when all accepted safety constraints are met; before real-data use the implementer presents the production reopening procedure for the product owner's approval. A procedure needing a product change awaits separate approval. After safe reopening, a SuperAdmin may re-enter lost business facts only through existing product commands, from supplied evidence and within accepted role authority. Engineering does not re-enter other business facts. Operators do not own system restore or interval reconstruction.

**B41 — Verification.** Before substantial consumers, settle and review the shared identifiers, fact ownership, state/control meanings, guard/atomic boundaries, operation/history shape, version bindings, owner interfaces and facility seams with tests for their invariants. This is an internal foundation check; continue when it passes.
