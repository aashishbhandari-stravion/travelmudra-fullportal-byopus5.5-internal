Max wants the TravelMudra portal premium, distinctive and state of the art, but with priority clarity + trust + workflow speed > visual spectacle. No generic AI-SaaS look, gratuitous gradients, excess cards or decorative motion. Every screen answers: where am I, what is happening, what needs my attention, what happens next.

**Why:** complex visa/application state, documents and money need trust and legibility; Max explicitly said not to stop for visual approval or adjudication of normal UX decisions.

**How to apply:** establish the design system (tokens, type, spacing, colour semantics, state patterns, components) in the foundation and reuse it; use the three skills installed 2026-10-04 at ~/.claude/skills (ui-ux-pro-max, impeccable, design-taste-frontend) as complementary judgment, with Claude as final authority; do one bounded final UI/UX pass after the functional frontend; never polish indefinitely. Impeccable engine 0.1.11 installed and verified on Max's explicit authorization (2026-10-04); agents may run its context and detect commands only — never live mode, update/install/link/pin, or CSP relaxation.
